The Forma Method: From Dental Findings to a Clear Plan

Transcript accompanies the approved October 15, 2026 edition.

Introducing the Forma Method. The Forma Method is a comprehensive health evaluation that uses your mouth as a window into your overall health, sleep, inflammation, and longevity. Instead of looking at one tooth at a time, we look at the complete picture. We ask, why is this happening? What caused this? What can we learn from it? And how can we help prevent future problems? The goal is not simply healthier teeth, the goal is a healthier you. Phase one, we call this discover. This is where we gather information. We use digital photography, intraoral scanning, facial scanning, CBCT imaging, comprehensive health histories, and sleep airway screening. This gives us an incredibly detailed understanding of your health. Think of it like creating a blueprint. Before building a home, you need plans. Before creating a personalized health strategy, we need data. So why this matters? Most people have never seen their oral health this way. Many patients are surprised when they learn their grinding may be related to airway issues. Their worn teeth may be connected to sleep problems. Their inflammation may be affecting more than just their gums. Their jaw position may be contributing to headaches or discomfort. Their oral bacteria may be influencing overall health. The earlier we identify these issues, the more options we have to address them. Phase two, understand. Data by itself doesn't improve health. Understanding does. After gathering information, we review everything together. We show you exactly what we're seeing. We explain what's healthy, what's concerning, what may need attention, and why it matters. Our patients often tell us this is the first time they've truly understood what's happening in their mouth. No confusing terminology, no pressure, just clarity. Phase 3. Personalized Health Plan. Every patient is different. That's why every Forma Method plan is customized. Your plan may include preventative care, airway improvements, sleep evaluation, bite optimization, gum therapy, smile enhancement, nutritional recommendations, and long -term health monitoring. The goal is simple. Help you maintain your health, health, function, appearance, and quality of life for decades to come. The Longevity Connection One of the reasons patients are so excited about the Forma Method is because it aligns with a growing interest in longevity. People want more than treatment. They want prevention. They want answers. They want to age well. The mouth provides valuable clues about what's happening throughout the rest of the body. By identifying concerns earlier, we can often help patients make better decisions before or problems become larger.
