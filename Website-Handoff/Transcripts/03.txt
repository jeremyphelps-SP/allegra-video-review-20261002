Composite bonding versus porcelain veneers. What is the technical difference? Composite is a tooth -colored resin that we apply and sculpt directly onto the tooth. It can often be done very conservatively and gives us a lot of control chair side. Porcelain veneers are custom -made ceramic restorations that are bonded to the teeth. They typically require some preparation, but they offer excellent color stability, stain resistance, and a very durable surface. composite is generally easier to repair, while porcelain tends to be more resistant to staining and wear. Neither is actually better, it really comes down to your tooth structure, your bite, your goals, and the specific case. Please reach out for a consultation at allegradc.com
