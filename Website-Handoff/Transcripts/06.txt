Bonding or Veneers: Which Fits Your Smile Goals?

Transcript accompanies the approved October 15, 2026 edition.

Composite bonding or porcelain veneers. Which one is right for you? They can both transform your smile, but they're very different treatments. Composite bonding is sculpted directly to your teeth and is typically a more conservative option. Porcelain veneers are custom -made and bonded to the front of your teeth. So which is better? It really depends on your teeth, your bite, and the look you're trying to achieve. We would love to see you for our personal consultation and help you decide which option is right for your smile.
