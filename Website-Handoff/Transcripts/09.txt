Before Veneers: What Makes a Smile Look Natural?

Transcript accompanies the approved October 15, 2026 edition.

I'm Dr. Dickson. Many people think cosmetic dentistry starts with whitening, bonding, or veneers. We believe it starts somewhere else. It starts with understanding why a smile looks the way it does. Sometimes teeth appear short, uneven, worn, or unbalanced. Not because of teeth themselves, but because of the relationship between the lips, gums, facial structure, and supporting tissues. If those elements aren't in harmony, simply changing the teeth rarely creates the most natural or lasting result. That's why we created the Forma Method, a thoughtful, structured approach to smile design that focuses on balance first and aesthetic second. Every smile begins with a comprehensive digital evaluation. We study facial proportions, tooth display, gum position, smile symmetry, and your personal goals to understand the complete picture before recommending treatment. When needed, we refine the foundation surrounding the smile through precise gum and tissue contouring, creating balance, symmetry, and proper proportions. Only then do we consider cosmetic enhancements such as whitening, bonding, or veneers. The result is a smile that feels natural, healthy, and uniquely yours, not overdone, not trendy, and never one -size -fits -all. At Allegra Dental Center, we don't simply enhance smiles. We restore balance first. That's the Forma Method, and we'd be honored to help you discover what's possible for your smile.
