Review transcript

The problem with traditional dentistry. Most dental visits follow the same pattern. You come in, someone counts your gums, looks for cavities, maybe takes some x -rays, then you leave. The problem is that many of the most important health issues don't begin as cavities. They begin much earlier. Many people are living with poor sleep, airway issues, chronic inflammation, grinding, jaw dysfunction, oral bacterial imbalances, and early signs of systemic disease. Unfortunately, these issues often go unnoticed for years, and the result, people spend years treating symptoms without understanding the root cause. That's where the Forma Method is different. Introducing the Forma Method. The Forma Method is a comprehensive health evaluation that uses your mouth as a window into your overall health, sleep, inflammation, and longevity. Instead of looking at one tooth at a time, we look at the complete picture. We ask, why is this happening? What caused this? What can we learn from it? And how can we help prevent the problems? The goal is not simply healthier teeth. The goal is a healthier you.
