Review transcript

Could composite bonding be right for you? If you have chipped teeth, small gaps, uneven edges, or you're just not happy with the shape or proportions of your smile, composite bonding might be a great option. But cosmetic dentistry is not one size fits all. We also want to look at your bite, your natural tooth structure, and things like grinding or clenching. The goal is to create a smile that looks natural, fits your face, and functions well. So, if you're considering bonding, the best place to start is with a consultation to see if the right option is for you. You can reach us via phone or you can find us on the website allegradc.com.

About the imagery
