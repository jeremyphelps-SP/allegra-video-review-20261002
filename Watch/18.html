Review transcript

What if your dentist could tell you more about your health than your teeth? Hi, I'm Dr. Dickson. Let me ask you a question. When was the last time your dentist talked to you about your sleep? Or inflammation? Or breathing? Or how your oral health might affect the rest of your body? The Forma Method is a comprehensive health evaluation that uses your mouth as a window into your overall health, sleep, inflammation, and longevity. Instead of looking at one tooth at a time, we look at the complete picture. We ask, why is this happening? What caused this? What can we learn from it? And how can we help prevent future problems? The goal is not simply healthier teeth. The goal is a healthier you. You want a dental office where you feel comfortable. You want a team that listens. You want recommendations you can trust. And you want to know that the people caring for you genuinely have your best interest at heart. That's exactly the type of practice we've worked hard to create. Phase one, we call this discover. This is where we gather information. We use digital photography, intraoral scanning, facial scanning, CBCT imaging, comprehensive health histories, and sleep airway screening. This gives us an incredibly detailed understanding of your health. Think of it like creating a blueprint. Before building a home, you need plans. Before creating a personalized health strategy, we need data. So why this matters? Most people have never seen their oral health this way. Many patients are surprised when they learn their grinding may be related to airway issues. Their worn teeth may be connected to sleep problems. Their inflammation may be affecting more than just their gums. Their jaw position may be contributing to headaches or discomfort. Their oral bacteria may be influencing overall health. The earlier we identify these issues, the more options we have to address them. If your jaw is sore, tight, clicking, or just feels tired all the time, you might assume you have TMJ. But TMJ is actually the name of the joint, the temporomandibular joint. And there are several different things that can cause pain in this area. Many people clench their teeth throughout the day without even realizing it. Others may grind or clench while they're sleeping. That can overwork the muscles around the jaw and the temples. The important thing is that your jaw pain isn't one diagnosis and the right treatment depends on identifying what's actually causing it. If your jaw pain is persistent, your jaw's locking, or you're having difficulty opening comfortably, that's a good reason to have it evaluated. Phase two, understand. Data by itself doesn't improve health. Understanding does. After gathering information, we review everything together. We show you exactly what we're seeing. We explain what's healthy, what's concerning, what may need attention, and why it matters. Our patients often tell us this is the first time they've truly understood what's happening in their mouth. No confusing terminology, no pressure, just clarity. Whether you're coming in for a routine cleaning, have concerns about your dental health, or you're interested in improving your smile, our goal is always the same, to help you feel informed, comfortable, and confident in your care. Phase three, personalized health plan. Every patient is different. That's why every Forma Method plan is customized. Your plan may include preventative care, airway improvements, sleep evaluation, bite optimization, gum therapy, smile enhancement, nutritional recommendations, and long-term health monitoring. The goal is simple. Help you maintain your health, function, appearance, and quality of life for decades to come. The longevity connection. One of the reasons patients are so excited about the Forma Method is because it aligns with a growing interest in longevity. People want more than treatment. They want prevention. They want answers. They want to age well. The mouth provides valuable clues about what's happening throughout the rest of the body. By identifying concerns earlier, we can often help patients make better decisions before problems become larger. Our philosophy is simple. Treat every patient the way we would want our own family treated. That means taking the time to get to know you, understand your goals, answer your questions, and help you make confident decisions about your health. We don't believe in rushing people through appointments. We don't believe in pressuring patients into treatment. We want you to feel welcomed, respected, and supported every step of the way. I invite you to experience a Forma Method for yourself. During your visit, we'll perform a comprehensive evaluation, review our findings together, and help you better understand your health, your risks, and your opportunities for improvement. Thank you for spending this time with me. My team and I look forward to meeting you and helping you discover what's possible through the Forma Method. Schedule your Forma Method health evaluation today.

About the imagery

The doctors are shown in their original recorded performances, with the previously approved appearance and presentation. Added illustrative scenes include AI-generated people, smiles and objects, plus existing generated films. They are not actual Allegra Dental Center patients, practice equipment or treatment outcomes.

The team photos and cover photography are from the approved dedicated photo collection. This edition was approved after Jeremy’s review with Dr. Dickson.
